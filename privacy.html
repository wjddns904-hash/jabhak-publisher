Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jabhak Multiplatform Publisher handles information when a user enables creator, AI, analytics, or publishing features.

1. Information the App may process

  • Account authorization data: OAuth access tokens, account identifiers, granted permissions, and basic account data returned by connected platforms.
  • Creator content: videos, audio, images, scripts, titles, captions, hashtags, metadata, and publishing settings.
  • Project and performance data: local project files, publishing records, and performance/analytics snapshots where enabled.
  • AI inputs and outputs: prompts, research requests, scripts, media instructions, generated text, audio, images, and related content.
  • API credentials: API keys or tokens entered by the user for enabled services.

2. How information is used

Information is processed only as needed to provide creator functions requested by the user, including authentication, content creation, rendering, upload/publishing, analytics, and local project management.

3. Local storage

The current desktop architecture stores project data and credentials primarily on the user's own device. API keys and platform access tokens may be stored in local configuration or token files.

4. Third-party processing

  • TikTok: account authorization and creator-selected video draft/upload operations.
  • Meta / Instagram: account authorization and Reels publishing operations.
  • Google / YouTube: authorization, upload, channel, and analytics operations.
  • OpenAI: AI-assisted research, scripting, image generation, audio generation/transcription, and other enabled AI features.

Each third party processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. TikTok data

When a user authorizes TikTok, the App may receive an access token, TikTok account identifier, and permissions granted by the user. This data is used only for authorized TikTok functions such as sending creator-selected video content to TikTok as a draft using the Content Posting API.

6. No sale of personal data

The App does not sell personal data and does not use connected-platform authorization data to build advertising profiles.

7. Retention

Locally stored data remains on the user's device until the user deletes the relevant files, folders, credentials, or application data. Third-party services may retain data according to their own policies.

8. Data deletion and revocation

Users can revoke platform access in the connected platform's settings and delete locally stored access tokens or application data. See Data Deletion Instructions.

9. Security

Reasonable precautions are used, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for securing their devices, accounts, API keys, and credentials.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for authorized creators and is not designed for children under 13. Users must also satisfy the minimum-age requirements of connected services.

11. International processing

Third-party service providers may process data in countries other than the user's country according to their own privacy notices.

12. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features, integrations, or legal requirements change.
